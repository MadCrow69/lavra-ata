# Lavra

A generator of Brazilian meeting minutes from the transcript, right in the browser: no server, with your own AI key, and with personal data masked before anything is sent.

[Português](README.md)

![Lavra demo: the corporate meeting example opens with the minutes ready; the "O que vai para a IA" (what goes to the AI) preview shows, side by side, the original CPF and the [CPF_1] marker sent in its place; finally, the PDF of the minutes is downloaded.](docs/demo.gif)

**Try it: https://madcrow69.github.io/lavra-ata/** — the "Ver exemplo" (see an example) button opens finished minutes of each type, without asking for a key and without sending anything to an AI.

The interface is in Brazilian Portuguese only, so button names are quoted in Portuguese, with a translation in parentheses the first time.

## How it works

1. You enter **your API key** (OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic), fill in the meeting details and the participants, and paste the transcript.
2. The app **masks personal identifiers** (CPF, CNPJ, phone, e-mail, card etc.) with [`@lavra/pii-br`](packages/pii-br/README.md) and shows, before sending, what goes to the AI ("Original" × "Enviado à IA", sent to the AI).
3. The call goes **straight from the browser to the provider**, without going through any Lavra server. If a provider fails, the app tries the next one in your priority list and records the switch in the section (e.g. "Gerado por Gemini · gemini-3.8-flash — OpenAI falhou (limite de uso)", generated by Gemini, OpenAI failed due to a usage limit).
4. The AI returns JSON validated by a schema; the app puts the original data back **in this browser** and lays out the minutes on an A4 sheet, as plain text (never HTML from the AI). You review section by section: edit by hand, regenerate, or adjust with an instruction.
5. With the minutes reviewed, you **download a PDF or DOCX** (with your logo in the header, if you want) or copy the tasks grouped by owner.

Design points:

- **The AI only writes what only the transcript knows.** Header, attendance list, participant tables, signatures, closing and draft notice come from the form and the minutes template, never from the AI; it cannot make up attendance, the presiding officers or the date.
- **Key in memory only** by default; it only goes to `localStorage` if you tick "Lembrar neste navegador" (remember in this browser). It never goes into a URL, the draft or an error message.
- **Low-cost default models** (the smallest of each provider: `gpt-6-luna`, `gemini-3.8-flash`, `claude-haiku-5-5`); you change them in the keys dialog.
- **Unit, ideal fraction and share of capital never go to the AI.** These fields (condominium and Ltda partners) stay in this browser and only go into the document.
- **Session draft** (`sessionStorage`): reloading the tab restores the form; the transcript and the generated minutes do not go to `localStorage`.
- **PDF and DOCX are built in the browser, with no network.** The generators ([pdfmake](https://pdfmake.github.io/docs/) and [docx](https://docx.js.org/), both MIT) are only loaded on the first click on "Baixar" (download), from the site's own origin; the fonts ([Inter](https://fontsource.org/fonts/inter) and [Source Serif 4](https://fontsource.org/fonts/source-serif-4), under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/)) are bundled in the app, with no CDN.
- **The logo never goes to the AI.** PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG (up to 2 MB), checked by its content before decoding; SVG is always turned into PNG and never inserted as markup.
- **Before downloading, the app warns** if `[PREENCHER]` (to be filled in) fields remain, if a section editor is still open and, for the PDF, which characters the bundled fonts cannot draw.

## Privacy: how to verify

You don't have to trust the text above: you can check it in your own browser. The app has this same page under the "Privacidade: como verificar" (privacy: how to verify) button, in the header.

**What leaves the browser.** Only the calls to the AI providers you registered, in the order of your list, with the transcript already masked. No Lavra server receives anything. The only allowed addresses are `api.openai.com`, `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` and `api.anthropic.com`.

**Check it in the Network tab.**

1. Open the developer tools: <kbd>F12</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+I</kbd> (on a Mac, <kbd>Cmd+Option+I</kbd>).
2. Go to the Network tab and reload the page.
3. Filter by Fetch/XHR.
4. Generate minutes: only calls to `api.openai.com`, `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` or `api.anthropic.com` appear, in the order of your list.
5. Open the call and look at what was sent (Payload): in place of CPF, phone and e-mail you see markers such as `[CPF_1]`.

**Check the security policy (CSP).** In the Elements tab, inside `<head>`, the `<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy">` tag lists the allowed destinations in `connect-src`; the browser itself blocks any other. The policy has `script-src 'self'` and `style-src 'self'`, with no `unsafe-inline` or `unsafe-eval`; it comes from a single definition, and the build checks the generated HTML against that definition and fails if they don't match.

**Check the code of this version.** The site footer shows the hash of the commit that produced the deployment, with a link to it; "Privacidade: como verificar" points to the code of that same commit. The site is published by GitHub Actions from `main`.

- AI adapters (the calls to the providers): [`apps/web/src/ai/providers`](apps/web/src/ai/providers)
- Detection and masking library: [`packages/pii-br/src`](packages/pii-br/src)
- CSP definition: [`apps/web/csp.config.js`](apps/web/csp.config.js)
- Workflow that publishes the site: [`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`](.github/workflows/deploy.yml)

**What is not protected.**

- People's names are not masked — the minutes need them.
- Sensitive information written in natural language (a diagnosis, an address, an opinion) goes as is.
- What is sent is subject to the terms of the provider you chose.
- A browser extension with access to the page can read the key and the text (see the [threat model](SECURITY.md)).
- Example mode sends nothing to an AI: the example ships ready-made with the site. Only the "Testar" (test) button in the keys dialog, if you click it, talks to the provider, to check your key, with nothing from the example.

## Minutes types and legal basis

- **Corporate meeting:** a working meeting of a company, team or committee; no legal form required.
- **CIPA:** an ordinary or extraordinary meeting of the Internal Commission for the Prevention of Accidents and Harassment, with each participant's representation and role (NR-5; Law No. 14,457/2022, art. 23).
- **Condominium:** an ordinary or extraordinary general meeting, in person, electronic or hybrid, with each owner's unit, relationship and ideal fraction (Civil Code, arts. 1,334 and 1,350 to 1,355; Law No. 4,591/1964).
- **Ltda partners:** a partners' meeting or assembly of a limited liability company, with the reported share of capital and representation by proxy (Civil Code, arts. 1,072 to 1,080-A; DREI's Manual for the Registration of Limited Liability Companies).

Each fixed text of the CIPA, condominium and Ltda templates points to the article or item of the rule, with an official link and the date it was consulted, in [`docs/base-legal.md`](docs/base-legal.md); an automated test fails any fixed text without a line there. **The app does not compute quorum, ideal fraction or the validity of a resolution:** votes and quorum go into the minutes only as they were declared in the meeting, and whatever was not said comes out as `[PREENCHER: …]` (to be filled in). In the Ltda minutes, the only calculation is the sum of the reported shares of the partners present, shown as "capital presente informado" (reported capital present).

## Run locally

Requires [pnpm](https://pnpm.io/installation); Node 24 is provisioned by the project itself.

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm --filter @lavra/web dev       # development server at http://localhost:5173/lavra-ata/
pnpm test                          # unit tests (Vitest, at most 4 workers)
```

To see the build with the CSP active (the development server does not inject it):

```bash
pnpm build
pnpm --filter @lavra/web preview   # http://localhost:4173/lavra-ata/
```

The app requires a **secure context** (HTTPS or `localhost`): it uses `crypto.randomUUID` and the clipboard, which the browser only allows in those contexts. Opened through a local network IP over HTTP, the app shows a notice explaining why.

The E2E tests (`pnpm --filter @lavra/web e2e`) run against the build and intercept the three providers: no real call goes out and no key is needed. The rest of the contribution workflow is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) (in Portuguese).

## Architecture

A pnpm monorepo with two packages: [`apps/web`](apps/web) (the app: Vite, React and TypeScript, a static site) and [`packages/pii-br`](packages/pii-br/README.md) (the Brazilian personal data library, in TypeScript with no dependencies). The flow of one generation:

```text
transcript ──► pii-br: mask ──► text with [CPF_1], [EMAIL_1]… ──► AI provider (direct fetch from the browser)
                    │                                                      │
                    └── marker → original map (in memory only)             ▼
form (details, participants) ─────────────────────────────────► JSON validated by schema
                                                                           │
          pii-br: checks the output and restores the originals ──► minutes template ──► sheet / PDF / DOCX
```

- **No backend:** the site is static (GitHub Pages) and the only connections are the browser's to the three providers.
- **CSP in a `<meta>` tag** (GitHub Pages does not allow custom headers), generated from a single source and checked at build time; the E2E tests run against the build with the CSP active and fail on any request to another host or any policy violation.
- **Local export:** PDF and DOCX are assembled in the browser, from the same model as the sheet.
- **Quality in CI:** formatting, lint, types, tests with coverage, build, dependency audit and E2E on every PR; CodeQL and OpenSSF Scorecard. As of 2026-10-22: 2,011 unit tests (Vitest) and 38 E2E tests (Playwright).

## Limitations

- **People's names are not masked.** The minutes need them; the library only masks identifiers with a format of their own (documents, contacts, card). This is not anonymization.
- **All minutes are drafts.** The minutes templates were written based on the cited legislation, **without review by a legal professional** (the basis of each fixed text is in [`docs/base-legal.md`](docs/base-legal.md)). Contributions from lawyers and practitioners are welcome ([How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **OpenAI straight from the browser:** it works (verified in the [connectivity spike](docs/spike-conectividade.md)), but OpenAI does not officially support this call, and CORS has broken before (Oct/2025 and Jan/2026, according to the research recorded in the [design document](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-lavra-design.md)). The provider priority list is the safeguard against a new break.
- **Anthropic adapter not verified with a real call.** It was built from the documentation and is tested with intercepted responses; the account used for testing has no credit. OpenAI and Gemini were verified in the spike.
- **PDF checked in parts.** For the corporate minutes, verified by opening PDFs with 200 participants and 200 action items in Chromium's viewer; for the CIPA, condominium (200 owners) and Ltda partners minutes, checked through the extracted text ("Página X de Y", page X of Y, on every page) and the first and last pages rendered, not in the viewer.
- **DOCX: typography not checked.** Fonts are not embedded in the DOCX and Word replaces them with substitutes. The layout was checked through the XML (repeated table header, rows that don't split, page numbering and image) and, for the CIPA, condominium and Ltda partners minutes, by opening the files in Microsoft Word: there the grid, columns, page breaks and signatures were checked, not the typography. The corporate minutes DOCX and the logo in Word are still checked through the XML only; LibreOffice was not tested.
- **Tasks by owner** are grouped ignoring only spacing, case and Unicode form: "Ana" and "Ana Souza" remain different people.
